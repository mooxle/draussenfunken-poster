We’re licensed radio amateurs – “hams” – on the air from the park today, all official. Here are the answers to the questions we hear most.

How to become a ham
1Learn
Courses, books and apps – many are free, and local clubs are happy to help.
2Exam
A short exam on technology, operating and rules – run by your national authority.
3Callsign
Pass it and you get your own callsign – and can go on the air yourself.
Most countries offer an entry-level licence – a great way to get started.
Quick questions
Is this CB radio?
No, something different: we may build our own gear, experiment and transmit with more power – that takes an exam.
Can I listen in?
Sure! Have a seat – we’re happy to show you how a radio contact works.
