DraußenfunkenDeutsch·Amateur radio under open skies

Draußenfunkenat a glance.

Six A4 posters to hang up, hand out and carry along – for passers-by, club evenings and the backpack. Draußenfunken is German for “radio from outside”. Free to print, improved together with the Draussenfunker community.

Get involved

Just do it – and make it better together.

1

Print

Open the PDF, print on A4, hang it up or put it in your backpack. Margins “none”, background graphics on.

2

Give feedback

Found a mistake, got an idea for a new poster? Open an issue – no Git knowledge needed.

3

Improve it

The posters are plain HTML. Edit, check in the browser, send a pull request.

No contest. Nobody loses.
